# User Guide - Swimming Time Converter

## Getting Started

The Swimming Time Converter is designed to be simple and intuitive. This guide walks you through all the features and how to use them effectively.

## Main Interface

The converter has three main sections:

### 1. Event Selection
Choose which swimming event you want to convert times for:
- **Freestyle**: 50 Free, 100 Free, 200 Free
- **Backstroke**: 50 Back, 100 Back, 200 Back
- **Breaststroke**: 50 Breast, 100 Breast, 200 Breast
- **Butterfly**: 50 Fly, 100 Fly, 200 Fly
- **Individual Medley (IM)**: 100 IM, 200 IM

### 2. Pool Format Selection

Select your current pool format and the format you want to convert to:

**SCY (Short Course Yards)**
- 25-yard pools
- Common in the United States high schools and colleges
- Used for many USA Swimming competitions

**SCM (Short Course Meters)**
- 25-meter pools
- Common in Europe and many countries worldwide
- Used in various international competitions

**LCM (Long Course Meters)**
- 50-meter pools
- Olympic standard
- Used in major international competitions

### 3. Time Input & Conversion

1. Enter your time in the input field (in seconds)
2. Click the **Convert** button
3. View your converted time instantly

## Examples

### Example 1: Convert 100 Free from Yards to Meters
- **Event**: 100 Free
- **From**: SCY
- **To**: SCM
- **Time**: 60 seconds
- **Result**: ~67.2 seconds

### Example 2: Olympic Conversion
- **Event**: 200 Free

## Tips for Accurate Conversions

- **Be precise with your time**: Even small differences affect the result
- **Know your current pool**: Make sure you select the correct "From" format
- **Understand pacing**: Times in longer pools may vary more from conversions due to pacing differences
- **Use for estimation**: These conversions are estimates based on average performance data

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Why is my converted time so different?**
A: Conversion factors account for pacing changes between different pool lengths. Longer pools typically result in different split times.

**Q: Can I use this for all events?**
A: Yes! The converter supports freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, and individual medley events at various distances.

**Q: Are the conversions exact?**
A: No, conversions are estimates based on historical performance data. Actual individual results may vary based on technique, training, and other factors.
